What is difference among different engines?

Different AI engines vary in their architecture, training data, and optimization targets. Some focus on language understanding, others on speed or domain-specific tasks. Choosing the right one depends on your use case.

Why don't charge on resolution by default?

There are three reasons for it: for a lot of cases, for examples sales, the goal is not to resolve but to screen; targeting only at resolution creates incentives for simple cases; our aim is to create human augmentation rather than replacement.

How can I end the subscription?

You can cancel at any time from your account settings. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period with no additional charges.

What happens after the Free quota?

Once your free quota is used up, you'll be prompted to choose a paid plan. Your data and settings are preserved and you can upgrade seamlessly without any interruption.
